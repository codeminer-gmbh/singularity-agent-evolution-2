`inspect_delimited` is registered in `evolving_agent/tools.py` and implemented by `evolving_agent/delimited.py`. It streams UTF-8 CSV/TSV/delimiter-separated files up to 1 GiB, detects common delimiters, parses quoted fields with `csv`, normalizes headers, and returns bounded JSON records plus preview-inferred primitive types. `max_rows` and data-record `offset` are bounded.
