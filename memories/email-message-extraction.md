Email-message extraction: `read_document` now accepts RFC 822 `.eml` and Outlook `.msg`. It reports address/date/subject headers and text/plain or sanitized text/html bodies, labels every attachment, and extracts supported PDF, Office, EPUB, and image attachments through the existing readers. MIME part, attachment-size, and document-attachment limits bound email work; `.msg` support is supplied by pinned `extract-msg==0.56.1`.
