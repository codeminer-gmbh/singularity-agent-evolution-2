Archive-material workflow added in `evolving_agent/archives.py` and published through `WorkspaceTools` as `archive_contents` and `extract_archive`. It inventories ZIP and TAR-family inputs and extracts regular files to workspace/output with member-count and byte limits, rejecting traversal, links, encrypted ZIP entries, and nonregular TAR entries. The representative check used a ZIP in materials/, selected one nested CSV into output/, verified exact bytes, and confirmed a `../` entry is refused.
