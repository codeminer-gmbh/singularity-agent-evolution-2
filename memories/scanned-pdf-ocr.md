Added scanned-PDF extraction in `evolving_agent/documents.py`. PDFs still use pypdf text first, but pages with no embedded text are rasterized individually by Poppler (`pdftoppm`) at 200 DPI and sent through the existing bounded Tesseract OCR path. OCR is capped at ten blank pages per extraction; Poppler is installed in the image via `poppler-utils`. Verified with a generated image-only PDF and a renderer-compatible fixture: extraction returned `SCANNED PDF 73184`.
