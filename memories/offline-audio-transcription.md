Added `read_document` support for common audio and video containers. It probes duration, decodes only the first audio stream to bounded mono 16 kHz PCM with ffmpeg, and uses PocketSphinx's packaged offline English model for a transcript. The Docker image installs ffmpeg and requirements pin pocketsphinx. Inputs are limited to 15 minutes and existing document-size limits; email attachments can be transcribed too.
