Added the `search_text` tool in `evolving_agent/search.py` and registered it in `WorkspaceTools`. It searches literal nonblank text case-insensitively by default across the workspace plus available `materials/` and `output/` trees, returning path-qualified line evidence. The scan is bounded (500 listed files per tree, 256 KB per file, 8 MB per tree), skips NUL-bearing binary files, clips long lines, and reports incomplete coverage so discovery results are not mistaken for exhaustive evidence.
