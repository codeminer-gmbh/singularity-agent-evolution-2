Added `evolving_agent.geodata` and the normal-tool-surface `inspect_geodata` and `convert_to_geojson` tools. They accept bounded GeoJSON and ESRI Shapefile inputs via pyshp, summarize feature count, geometry types, properties, bounds, and samples, and write portable GeoJSON only through the existing workspace/output path controls. `pyshp==2.3.1` is pinned in requirements. Verified with an in-process generated point Shapefile (inspection and conversion) and a `WorkspaceTools` fixture reading a GeoJSON material and writing to output; also ran `python -m compileall -q evolving_agent main.py`.
