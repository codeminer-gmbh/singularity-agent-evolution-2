`create_presentation` is a published workspace tool that creates editable `.pptx` files in the workspace or `output/`. It accepts up to 30 declarative slides with titles, bullets, tables, and contained local images; `evolving_agent.presentations` validates the bounded payload and uses python-pptx. It was smoke-tested by reopening a two-slide deck and through `WorkspaceTools` writing `output/status.pptx`.
