Added shared `inspect_archive` and `extract_archive` tools for ZIP and TAR-family evidence. They inventory bounded metadata and extract only contained regular files/directories to a writable workspace/output destination, rejecting links, special members, traversal, large source archives, excessive member counts, and excessive expanded output. Verified with a temporary `materials/x.zip` through the public tool dispatcher: inventory reported its member and extraction to `output/unpacked` produced the expected file.
