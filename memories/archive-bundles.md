Added `inspect_archive` and `extract_archive` workspace tools backed by `evolving_agent/archives.py`. They handle ZIP, TAR (including compressed variants), and single-file GZIP inputs; inspection lists bounded nested manifests, and extraction supports exact member selection. The implementation rejects links, special TAR entries, duplicate or escaping paths, oversized archives/members, and destinations outside writable workspace/output trees. Verified with nested ZIP inspection, selective output extraction, TAR/GZIP extraction, traversal rejection, and WorkspaceTools materials-to-output routing.
