Archive document inputs are now supported by `extract_document`: ZIP plus TAR, TGZ, TAR.GZ, TAR.BZ2, and TAR.XZ produce a member-labelled manifest and extract common text or nested supported office/image documents. Reading is bounded to 60 files, 12 MiB per member and 50 MiB declared unpacked content; members are only written under a fresh temporary directory, so declared archive paths are never used for filesystem extraction. Archive formats are deliberately read-only and `create()` rejects them.
